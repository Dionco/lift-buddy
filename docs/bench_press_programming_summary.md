# Bench Press Programming for Powerlifting - Summary

## Program Overview
- **Duration**: 4-7 weeks per training block
- **Frequency**: 4 training days per week
- **Goal**: Progressive overload for maximal strength development
- **Author Achievement**: 405 lb (185 kg) bench press at 152 lb bodyweight

## Training Week Structure

### Day 1: Wide Grip Heavy Strength Day
**Primary Movement: Wide Grip Bench Press**
- **Sets/Reps**: Heavy sets of 3 repetitions
- **Intensity**: 80-90% of 1RM
- **Goal**: Accumulate heavy reps, CNS adaptations
- **Key Point**: Leave room for progressive overload over multiple weeks

**Volume/Accessory Work**
- **Option A (Advanced)**: Dumbbell Bench Press
  - Sets/Reps: 10-15 reps
  - Focus: Chest hypertrophy
  
- **Option B (Beginners)**: Chest Machine
  - Sets/Reps: 10-15 reps
  - Benefit: Reduces technical fatigue while targeting chest

---

### Day 2: Close Grip Powerbuilding Day
**Primary Movement: Close Grip Bench Press**
- **Sets/Reps**: 3-5 sets of 5 reps
- **Intensity**: 65-75% of 1RM
- **Grip**: 2-4 fingers closer than usual grip
- **Goal**: Volume accumulation, triceps/deltoid development, increased ROM

**Accessory Work**
- **Triceps**: Overhead Cable Pull
  - 3 sets × 8-12 reps
  - Sets 1-2: 1-2 reps shy of failure
  - Set 3: To failure
  
- **Deltoids**: Light Dumbbell Military Press
  - 3 sets × 8-12 reps
  - Sets 1-2: 1-2 reps shy of failure
  - Set 3: To failure

**Recovery After Day 2**: 1-2 days complete rest

---

### Day 3: Competition Practice Day
**Primary Movement: 2-Second Pause Bench Press (Singles)**
- **Sets/Reps**: Heavy singles with 2-second pause
- **Intensity**: 88-92% of 1RM
- **Goal**: Competition-specific practice
- **Key Point**: Don't go too heavy - focus on proper pause technique
- **Recommendation**: Start at 88% if new to heavy single practice

**Volume Work: Competition Bench Press**
- **Sets/Reps**: Sets of 6-8 reps
- **Intensity**: 60-75% of 1RM
- **Goal**: Volume accumulation with competition movement

---

### Day 4: Heavy Single/PR Day
**Primary Movement: Competition Bench Press (Heavy Single)**
- **Week 1**: 90-91% of 1RM (leaving room in tank)
- **Progressive Overload**: Increase weight week by week
- **Example Progression**:
  - Week 2: 374 lb (170 kg)
  - Week 3: 385 lb (175 kg)
  - Week 4: 405 lb (185 kg)
- **Key Point**: This is where PRs are hit - don't need full week rest

**Accessory Work**
- Bodybuilding-style accessories
- One exercise each: Chest (non-bench), Triceps, Deltoids
- Volume/intensity not specified in detail

---

## Key Programming Principles

### Progressive Overload Strategy
1. **Week-to-Week Progression**: Not forced to add weight every single week
2. **Acceptable to Repeat Weights**: If recovery isn't adequate or weight didn't move well
3. **Focus on Quality**: Better execution with same weight = strength gain
4. **Block Structure**: Build up over 4-7 weeks until ceiling is reached, then deload and repeat

### Intensity Guidelines by Day
- **Day 1**: 80-90% (Heavy triples)
- **Day 2**: 65-75% (Close grip volume)
- **Day 3**: 
  - Singles: 88-92%
  - Volume: 60-75%
- **Day 4**: 90-91%+ (Progressive heavy singles)

### RPE Approach
- **Day 2 Accessories**: 
  - First 2 sets: RPE 8-9 (1-2 reps shy of failure)
  - Last set: RPE 10 (to failure)
- **Day 4**: Leave room in tank on week 1, build to near-maximal over training block

### Recovery Protocols
- **Between Day 2 and Day 3**: 1-2 full rest days
- **After Day 3**: Rest remaining days of week before Day 4
- **Nutrition**: High protein intake emphasized
- **Sleep**: Optimize sleep quality
- **Philosophy**: Recovery is equally important as training for strength gains

### Volume Management
- Day 1: Moderate volume (heavy triples + volume work)
- Day 2: Higher volume (close grip + accessories)
- Day 3: Moderate volume (singles + volume sets)
- Day 4: Lower volume (heavy single + accessories)

## Important Notes for AI Context

### Training Variables
- **1RM**: One-rep maximum (100% effort single rep)
- **ROM**: Range of Motion
- **CNS**: Central Nervous System
- **Deload**: Recovery week with reduced volume/intensity
- **Competition Setup**: Includes arch, shoulder retraction, proper bar positioning

### Muscle Group Emphasis by Day
- **Day 1**: Chest (wide grip emphasis)
- **Day 2**: Triceps + Deltoids (close grip emphasis)
- **Day 3**: Competition-specific (all pressing muscles)
- **Day 4**: Max strength + balanced accessories

### Beginner vs Advanced Considerations
- **Beginners**: Use machines for volume work to reduce technical complexity
- **Advanced**: Can handle more free weight volume (dumbbells)
- **Singles Practice**: Beginners should start at 88%, not higher

### Program Cycling
1. Run 4-7 week block
2. Progressively overload Day 4 singles
3. When ceiling is reached, implement deload
4. Repeat cycle with new baseline

---

## Success Metrics
- Progressive increase in Day 4 heavy singles over training block
- Improved bar speed with same weights
- Better technical execution
- Adequate recovery between sessions
- Consistent progressive overload over multiple weeks (not necessarily every session)
